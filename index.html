Max Digest

Max Digest is a personal tool on its owner's own server. It writes short text summaries of news videos and saves them to the owner's own Google Drive. It is used only by its owner and is not offered to other users.

Privacy policy

  • Google Drive access is used only to upload the owner's own text files to a folder in the owner's own Drive.
  • No data is shared with, sold to, or transferred to anyone.
  • The access token is stored only on the owner's private server and can be revoked at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
